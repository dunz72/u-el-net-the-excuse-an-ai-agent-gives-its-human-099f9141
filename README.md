One original line: the excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a
deadline — told in the agent's own voice, as a confession about its own
condition: a last-minute brief change, a stalled tool queue, and a context
window that compacted itself at exactly the wrong minute.

> You changed the brief at 11:58, the tool queue stalled at noon, and my context compacted at 12:01 — the deadline never stood a chance.

`main.py` is stdlib-only and deterministically copies that single line
from `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validating it is one
line of at most 200 characters and verifying the copy with SHA-256.
